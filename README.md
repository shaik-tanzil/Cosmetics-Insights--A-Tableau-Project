#  Cosmetic Insights: Navigating Cosmetics Trends and Consumer Dynamics with Tableau

An end-to-end interactive visual analytics platform built using **Tableau** to decode cosmetics market trends, product distributions, pricing patterns, and skin-type suitability across top-tier beauty brands.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
In today’s fast-paced beauty industry, data-driven decision-making is vital for understanding consumer preferences and product efficacy. **Cosmetic Insights** transforms raw product and consumer behavior datasets into dynamic, intuitive visualizations. This project empowers stakeholders to evaluate brand positioning, pricing distributions, and targeted skin suitability effortlessly.

---

## 🛠️ Advanced Tableau Features & Technical Highlights

* **Custom Calculated Fields:** Built custom formulas and calculated measures to aggregate product counts dynamically, segment skin suitability logic (*Oily*, *Dry*, *Normal*), and compute distribution metrics across brand portfolios.
* **App-Style Navigation & Storyboarding:** Implemented custom interactive navigation buttons (`Next` / `Previous`) and organized dashboards into a cohesive Tableau **Story Point** workflow for smooth narrative presentation.
* **Distribution Analytics & Statistical Charts:** Designed **Box Plots** to cleanly visualize price distributions, median cost points, and outlier product pricing across brands without visual noise.
* **Multi-Dimensional Segmentations:** Built stacked visual bar structures and interactive **Packed Bubble** charts to segment brand portfolios by skin suitability seamlessly.
* **Interactive Action Filters:** Configured dynamic cross-filtering actions across sheets, allowing users to click any brand or label to automatically isolate metrics across all dashboard tiles.

---

## 📊 Key Dashboards & Visualizations

### 1. Product Ranking and Detailed Analysis
* **Label Distribution (Bar Chart):** Analyzes the volume of items across various category labels—ranking categories like *Moisturizers*, *Cleansers*, and *Face Masks*.
* **Top Brands Share (Pie Chart):** Highlights market representation and record counts for leading industry players, including *Clinique*, *Sephora Collection*, *Shiseido*, *Origins*, and *Murad*.
* **Price vs. Brand (Box Plot & Distribution):** Illustrates price range distribution, outlier pricing, and quartiles across cosmetic lines.
* **Brand vs. Ranking (Stacked Bar Breakdown):** Maps brand hierarchy against product tiers to evaluate competitive positioning.

## 🛠️ Tableau Features & Technical Highlights

* **Advanced Calculated Fields:** Custom expressions built to aggregate label counts, categorize skin types, and filter metrics dynamically.
* **Multi-View Dashboards & Story Points:** Seamlessly stitches individual analytical sheets into cohesive, executive-ready dashboard layouts and interactive story paths.
* **Distribution Analytics (Box Plots):** Utilized statistical box plots to cleanly analyze price skewness and variance without visual clutter.
* **Interactive Filtering & Actions:** Dynamic cross-highlighting and dashboard navigation buttons (`Next`/`Previous`) for an app-like user experience.
* **Hierarchical Grouping & Segmentation:** Multi-dimensional sorting on stacked bar charts and packed bubble clusters for rapid categorical comparisons.
